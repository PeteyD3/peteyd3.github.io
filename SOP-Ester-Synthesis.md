# Standard Operating Procedure: Synthesis and Isolation of Hexyl Acetate via Fischer Esterification
**Document ID:** SOP-SYN-688  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** August 31, 2026  
**Prepared By:** Peter Duncan, Technical Writer  

### 1.0 Purpose & Scope
This document defines the standard operating procedure for the acid-catalyzed synthesis of hexyl acetate from 1-hexanol and glacial acetic acid. This procedure outlines operational parameters for reflux initialization, liquid-liquid extraction, and final purification via simple distillation. This protocol applies to all chemical manufacturing and laboratory synthesis technicians.

### 2.0 Material & Equipment Specifications
Ensure all raw materials and chemical reagents meet processing requirements before initialization:
*   **Reactants:** 1-Hexanol (12.8 mL), Glacial Acetic Acid (13.48 mL).
*   **Catalyst / Process Aids:** Concentrated Sulfuric Acid (1.0 mL), Anhydrous Magnesium Sulfate, Boiling Stones.
*   **Wash Solutions:** Saturated Sodium Chloride, Saturated Sodium Bicarbonate.
*   **Hardware Setup:** 100 mL Round-Bottom Flask, West Condenser, Thermowell Heating Mantle, Variable Transformer, 125 mL Separatory Funnel, Simple Distillation Apparatus.

### 3.0 Safety & Protective Controls
> ⚠️ **CRITICAL HAZARD WARNING:** Concentrated sulfuric acid and glacial acetic acid are highly corrosive and cause severe chemical burns. Handle exclusively inside an active chemical fume hood.

Operators must wear the following Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) at all times:
*   Splash-resistant chemical safety goggles.
*   Heavy-duty nitrile laboratory gloves.
*   Heat-resistant gloves (required during distillation and thermal breakdown steps).

### 4.0 Step-by-Step Processing Procedure

#### 4.1 Reaction Initialization & Reflux Setup
1.  **Charge Reactants:** Quantitatively transfer 12.8 mL of 1-hexanol and 13.48 mL of glacial acetic acid into a clean 100 mL round-bottom flask.
2.  **Add Catalyst:** Carefully pipette 1.0 mL of concentrated sulfuric acid into the mixture, followed by exactly 4 boiling chips to prevent bumping.
3.  **Assemble Apparatus:** Mount the flask onto the Thermowell heating mantle. Attach the West condenser, ensuring all ground-glass joints are lightly lubricated with vacuum grease. Connect water lines so coolant flows from the bottom of the condenser upward.
4.  **Execute Reflux:** Energize the variable transformer to a setting of **30**. Once light boiling initiates (approximately 15–20 minutes), start a **45-minute** process timer.
5.  **Cooling Cycle:** De-energize the transformer after 45 minutes. Safely lower the Thermowell mantle and allow the reaction vessel to equilibrate to room temperature before disassembly.

#### 4.2 Purification via Liquid-Liquid Extraction
1.  **Decant Mixture:** Carefully pour the cooled reaction solution into a 125 mL separatory funnel, ensuring the stopcock is fully closed. Discard residual boiling stones.
2.  **Aqueous Brine Wash:** Charge the funnel with 25 mL of saturated sodium chloride. Stopper the funnel, invert it, and shake firmly. Open the stopcock immediately after each shake cycle to vent internal vapor pressure.
3.  **Phase Separation:** Mount the funnel right-side up, remove the glass stopper, and allow the bilayer to resolve fully. Drain the dense, bottom aqueous layer into a dedicated waste vessel. Repeat this brine wash cycle two additional times.
4.  **Neutralization Wash:** Wash the remaining organic top layer with two separate 25 mL portions of saturated sodium bicarbonate using identical inversion and venting techniques. Drain and collect all bottom aqueous layers for hazardous inorganic waste disposal.
5.  **Desiccant Drying:** Transfer the crude organic layer into a 50 mL Erlenmeyer flask. Add 3 scoops of anhydrous magnesium sulfate, swirling continuously until the desiccant powder floats freely, indicating complete moisture removal.

#### 4.3 Fractional Isolation via Simple Distillation
1.  **Filter & Charge Vessel:** Gravity filter the dried organic solution through fluted filter paper into a clean, pre-weighed 100 mL round-bottom flask. Add 4 fresh boiling stones.
2.  **Assemble Distillation Rig:** Connect the distillation head, thermometer (ensuring the bulb sits completely below the adapter arm junction), condenser, and vacuum adapter. Place a clean tared vial at the collection endpoint.
3.  **Execute Thermal Separation:** Set the variable transformer to a setting of **40**. 
4.  **Discard Forerun:** Collect the first 1.0 to 2.0 mL of initial distillate in a separate waste tube and discard it to eliminate residual non-ester fractions.
5.  **Collect Pure Fraction:** Swap the collection vessel out for your clean tared vial. Monitor and record the vapor temperature plateau near the targeted boiling parameter (~59°C).
6.  **Terminate Run:** Shut off power to the transformer when approximately 2.0 mL of liquid remains in the boiling flask to prevent thermal cracking or hazardous dry heating. Allow the system to cool completely.

### 5.0 Material Tracking & Quality Management
1.  **Yield Analysis:** Mass the collected distillate vial. Subtract the empty vial tare weight to compute the final net mass of pure hexyl acetate (Target Baseline: ~5.875 grams, representing a ~40% reaction yield).
2.  **Analytical Verification:** Submit 1 drop of the purified product into a clean NMR tube for spectroscopic structural confirmation. Verify the existence of a definitive singlet at 2.0 ppm (acetate methyl group) and a triplet at 4.0 ppm (O-CH2 link) to confirm successful ester generation.
